Items (each seen from the front):
1 top-left: the entrance gate of a Korean traditional town market: a wide gentle arch of pale wood and teal painted steel over a small street, a long blank cream signboard across the top of the arch, red paper lanterns hanging under it, small fruit and vegetable stalls with striped awnings on both sides.
2 top-right: a low painted village wall of a former coal-mining town: a cheerful mural painted on the wall shows a smiling coal miner in a yellow helmet with a headlamp holding a round metal lunch box, a small black mine cart full of coal and a neat stack of black coal briquettes.
3 bottom-left: a second low painted village wall with a warm mural: a coal miner father in work clothes walking home under the stars hand in hand with two small children, little houses on a hillside behind them.
4 bottom-right: a small Korean mountain-town railway station building: two storeys, pale grey walls, a dark blue tiled roof, a round clock, glass doors, a long blank cream signboard above the entrance.
