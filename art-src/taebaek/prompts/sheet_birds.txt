Items (birds of the Taebaek mountains, each seen from the side):
1 top-left: a Brown Dipper: a plump chocolate-brown songbird with a short cocked tail, standing on a wet grey stream stone.
2 top-right: a male Mandarin Duck swimming on a small ripple: orange sail feathers on the back, a white eye stripe, purple breast, red bill.
3 bottom-left: an Oriental Scops Owl: a small grey-brown owl with ear tufts and big yellow eyes, perched on a short branch.
4 bottom-right: a White-backed Woodpecker: black and white barred back, red crown, clinging to a short section of tree trunk.
