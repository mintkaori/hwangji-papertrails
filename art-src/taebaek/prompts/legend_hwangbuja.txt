Items (four storybook scenes of a Korean folk tale, each scene a separate little cut-out vignette standing on its own small paper ground plate, simple and readable for children, no frightening details):
1 top-left: an old Buddhist monk with a wooden alms bowl at the gate of a rich tiled-roof Korean house; a stingy rich old man in hanbok scoops cow dung into the bowl.
2 top-right: the kind young daughter-in-law in hanbok secretly offering a gourd dipper full of white rice to the monk, bowing politely.
3 bottom-left: the daughter-in-law with a baby on her back walking up a mountain path behind the monk, turning to look back over her shoulder while lightning flashes behind her.
4 bottom-right: on a mountain ridge a grey stone figure of the woman with the baby on her back stands quietly, while below a round blue pond has appeared where the rich house was.
