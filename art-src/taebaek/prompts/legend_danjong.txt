Items (four storybook scenes of a Korean folk tale, each scene a separate little cut-out vignette on its own small paper ground plate, gentle and calm, readable for children):
1 top-left: a young Joseon boy king in a red royal robe and a black winged hat, looking thoughtfully at a river from a small pavilion in the mountains.
2 top-right: the boy king riding a white horse along a snowy mountain path.
3 bottom-left: a kind old official in white hanbok holding up a basket of wild dark grapes to the boy king on the white horse.
4 bottom-right: the boy king on the white horse standing peacefully on a snowy summit beside an old yew tree and a round stone altar, a soft glow around him.
