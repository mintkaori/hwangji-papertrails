Items (four storybook scenes of a Korean folk tale, each scene a separate little cut-out vignette on its own small paper ground plate, gentle and readable for children):
1 top-left: a white dragon in a mountain stream on one side of a tall layered rock wall.
2 top-right: a blue dragon and the white dragon facing each other in a playful tussle on top of the tall rock wall.
3 bottom-left: the white dragon boring through the bottom of the rock wall, making a round hole with river water rushing through it.
4 bottom-right: a layered stone arch with a river flowing through its round hole, and the white dragon flying up into the sky above it.
