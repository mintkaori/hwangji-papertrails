Subject: Gumunso in Taebaek, a natural monument: a large rock mountain of layered grey, slate and ochre limestone with a big natural round tunnel arch through its base where a turquoise river flows out, clearly visible horizontal sedimentary strata stripes with a few tiny trilobite fossils, a few small pine trees and autumn shrubs on top. Front elevation, the whole rock as one cut-out piece with margin, about 1.5 times wider than tall.
