Items (Korean freshwater fish of the cold mountain streams of Taebaek, each seen from the side in a lively swimming pose):
1 top-left: Kumgang fat minnow (geumgangmochi): a small slender fish, olive-brown back, a golden-orange stripe along the side, silvery belly.
2 top-right: Chinese minnow (beodeulchi): a small slender olive-brown fish with tiny dark speckles and a rounded tail.
3 bottom-left: Korean sculpin (dukjunggae): a stout fish with a big flat head, wide fan-like pectoral fins, mottled brown with dark bands.
4 bottom-right: Korean slender catfish (miyugi): a long slim grey-brown catfish with two long whiskers and a long low fin along the belly.
