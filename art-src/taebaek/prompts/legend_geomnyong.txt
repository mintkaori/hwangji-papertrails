Items (four storybook scenes of a Korean folk tale, each scene a separate little cut-out vignette on its own small paper ground plate, gentle and readable for children):
1 top-left: a long wingless water serpent (imugi) with a friendly face swimming in the waves of the sea near a small island.
2 top-right: the serpent swimming up a long winding river between green mountains.
3 bottom-left: the serpent wriggling up a narrow rocky gorge with a small waterfall, its body carving curving channels into the rock.
4 bottom-right: a small round turquoise spring pool among mossy rocks in a forest, and a dragon rising from it into the clouds.
