Items (Taebaek mountain trees, each a standing tree seen from the front):
1 top-left: a Japanese yew tree (Taxus cuspidata, the city tree of Taebaek): dense dark green needled crown in irregular layered clumps, thick twisted reddish-brown trunk.
2 top-right: an ancient dead yew snag from the Taebaeksan summit: twisted silver-grey trunk and bare gnarled branches coated with white rime frost and a little snow.
3 bottom-left: a Khingan fir tree (Abies nephrolepis): narrow dark blue-green conical fir with snow resting on its layered branches.
4 bottom-right: an Erman's birch tree: crooked pale cream-pink peeling bark trunk with light yellow-green leaf clusters.
